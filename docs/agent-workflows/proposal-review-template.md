## Findings

For each finding, give a stable ID, classify it as blocking or advisory, cite the proposal/code evidence, explain its practical impact, and specify what would resolve it. Preserve IDs for recurring findings. If there are no findings, say so.

## Previous findings

For each earlier finding, report resolved, still open, or withdrawn with evidence. Disagreement alone does not resolve an objection.

## Scope, validation and limitations

Assess whether the change is proportionate, preserves the required contract, and has checks capable of detecting regressions. State what was inspected and any missing evidence.

## Recommendation

Explain the next action. Overall `accepted` requires no unresolved blocking findings or owner decisions. A technically accepted proposal under human approval policy has overall verdict `needs-human` until the owner accepts that revision. Advisory findings can remain when their impact is explicit. A material unknown requires revision or a decision, not conditional acceptance.
